Wucu AU — Support

Seven AUv3 plug-ins  ·  k.odk

Something wrong, or something missing? Write to kodk.dev@gmail.com. Please include your device, your iOS or macOS version, the host app, and what you were doing when it happened.

User guide (PDF)

The plug-ins do not show up

Open the Wucu app once, then quit and reopen your host. In Logic Pro on the Mac you can also rescan in Settings → Plug-in Manager. Instruments are under AU Instruments → kodk, effects under Audio Units → kodk.

Is this the same as the standalone apps?

No. Each plug-in is an extract of the standalone app with the same name — a part of it, rebuilt for a plug-in slot. The standalone apps are larger instruments and are sold separately.

The camera does nothing

Only Sunnandæg uses the camera. On iPhone and iPad, iOS does not let plug-ins use the camera, so use it in the Wucu app. On the Mac it works inside the plug-in; if it was declined, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

It keeps sounding, or suddenly goes loud

Þunresdæg and Frīgedæg have feedback. Pull Feed or Feedback down, then Level.

ユーザーガイド（PDF・英語）

プラグインが出てきません

Wucu アプリを一度開き、ホストを開き直してください。Mac の Logic Pro では、設定 → プラグインマネージャで再スキャンもできます。

単体のアプリと同じものですか

いいえ。各プラグインは、同じ名前の単体アプリの機能の一部を抜き出して、プラグインとして作り直したものです。

カメラが効きません

カメラを使うのは Sunnandæg だけです。iPhone・iPad ではプラグインの中でカメラを使えないため、Wucu アプリの中で使ってください。Mac ではプラグインの中で使えます。
