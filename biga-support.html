Biga — Support

Two circuits, played by drawing  ·  k.odk

Something wrong, or something missing? Write to kodk.dev@gmail.com. Please include your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened — it makes a fix much more likely.

How do I play it?

Draw shapes over the contacts on the board: wherever a shape lies, the circuit is touched there and answers. Press RANDOM, then the red scribble left of PLAY, and raise VOLUME. The full user guide (PDF) covers every key: Biga_iOS_UserGuide.pdf

Nothing is playing

Check the silent switch and VOLUME first. A circuit only sounds where shapes touch its contacts, and only through its outputs (L, R): press RANDOM and draw a few shapes. STARVE all the way down starves the circuit silent.

It changes by itself

That is deliberate: flickering and swaying shapes move over new contacts. Draw with flicker and sway off (◌ – and ◉ –) for shapes that stay still.

The sound keeps going after I leave the app

Also deliberate: it keeps playing while you switch apps or lock the screen, the way an instrument should. To stop it, lower VOLUME or close the app.

Can I export the sound?

Use Biga as an AUv3 instrument in a host that records, use the iOS screen recorder, or run the output into a recorder through an audio interface.
