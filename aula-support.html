Aula for AUv3 — Support

An AUv3 host, played on a field  ·  k.odk

Something wrong, or something missing? Write to kodk.dev@gmail.com. Please include your device model, your iOS version, the plug-ins you had loaded, and what you were doing when it happened — it makes a fix much more likely.

How do I start?

Tap a track's empty source and choose an instrument — an AUv3 on your device, or Aula's own drum machine. Add effects in the places around it. Open PLAY and draw a circle with two fingers over the sources to catch them; double-tap the circle to choose how it plays.

A plug-in does not appear

Open the plug-in's own app once, then restart Aula. Only AUv3 plug-ins are listed; MIDI-processor apps appear in a PLAY circle's chooser, not in a track.

A plug-in crashed or went silent

Aula hosts plug-ins made by others, and some may be unstable with it. Remove the plug-in and add it again, or restart Aula. If it keeps happening, please tell us which plug-in it was.

Nothing is playing

Check the silent switch, the track's VOL and MUTE, and the master level. In PLAY, a circle only plays the sources it has caught.

The sound keeps going after I leave the app

That is deliberate: Aula keeps playing while you switch apps or lock the screen, the way an instrument should. To stop it, stop playback or close the app.
